## Edit 2: the behavior-problem DM

His note: *"Honestly, I probably wouldn't use the skill for this at all. I'd do a face to face
conversation where I assume best intent, lead with curiosity, and say the thing."*

The draft was five paragraphs: a long grant of the person's speed, the impact, a prescribed fix ("So

replacement, in full:

> Hey sir,
>
> I've gotten some feedback about the shared demo environment that I'd like to understand. To say the
> thing, it got claimed twice this week during someone else's release window, and it's the second time
> that has happened. Obviously, those folks are pretty annoyed that they weren't given a heads up.

*One paragraph instead of five. The grant is one clause, not a paragraph. Other people's annoyance
stated as plain fact rather than abstracted into process language. **No prescribed remedy**. It ends on
two genuine questions with a small concrete ask embedded ("an hour or two").*

## Edit 4: affirmation, and how he offers to take something on

From a DM to someone who kept absorbing requests after their sprint was planned.

> ~~I know you're trying to help, and honestly that's one of the things I like about you. But I don't
> think you should be absorbing that by yourself. Who's sending these, and is that something I should be
> handling instead of you?~~
>
> I know you're trying to help, and I love that. But I don't think you should be putting that on
> yourself. Who's sending these, and is that something I can handle to free you up?

*Three separate corrections, and the third is the most important.*

*"honestly that's one of the things I like about you" becomes "I love that." The original is an appraisal
of the person and reads like a performance review. His is three words, warmer, and about the thing they
did rather than about them.*

*"absorbing that by yourself" becomes "putting that on yourself." Plainer verb, closer to speech.*

*"is that something I should be handling instead of you?" becomes "is that something I can handle to free
you up?" The original reallocates work away from them, which carries a quiet implication that they were
doing it wrong. His version puts the benefit on them. Same offer, opposite direction.*
